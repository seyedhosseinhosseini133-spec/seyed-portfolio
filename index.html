About me

My Life

Hi, I’m Seyed Hossein Hosseini. I discovered computers at age 6 and taught myself to build a calculator app from a tutorial video, without any classes. I started professional programming at 7, and I’ve been coding for 9 years now — learning many languages along the way, some of which you can see in my skills.

Interests

My main passion is programming — it’s how I relax, play and live. I love the movie The Social Network (highly recommended!), mathematics for the way it challenges the mind, and games like Sudoku and chess. If I split my life into two parts: programming and Hey’at (my religious gatherings).

Journey and Goals

Age doesn’t matter — whenever you start and put in the effort, success is yours. Always try to create what no one else has thought of, like Steve Jobs, who cared about beauty at a time when nobody else did. Most people have limited minds — try not to be one of them.
